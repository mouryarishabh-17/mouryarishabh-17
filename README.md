## 🎯 About Me

I design and build AI-powered software that turns business challenges into practical, scalable solutions.

My work spans applied AI, backend engineering, intelligent automation, APIs, and data infrastructure. I enjoy building systems that integrate LLMs, automation workflows, and modern software engineering to improve business operations and user experiences.

I've built projects ranging from AI-powered analytics platforms and real-time data pipelines to automation workflows, with a focus on delivering reliable, end-to-end solutions rather than isolated features.

I'm interested in opportunities where I can work closely with customers, understand complex problems, and rapidly build software that creates measurable business impact particularly in Applied AI, AI Automation, Forward Deployed Engineering, and GTM Engineering.

I enjoy collaborating with teams that value ownership, rapid iteration, and solving meaningful problems through technology.
